Sumsmith

Calm kakuro (cross sums) puzzles with big, clear grids. Every puzzle can be solved by logic, with no guessing.

  • Big, clear digits and a large number pad, plus light, dark and high-contrast themes
  • Grids sized to your screen, with zoom for the big ones
  • A new daily puzzle, hints that explain the next step, no timers
  • Works offline. No account needed

Available on Google Play and the App Store.
