Help & contact

How do I play?

Fill every white square with a digit from 1 to 9. The number in a dark square is a clue: the digits in the run to its right (top number) or below it (bottom number) add up to it. A digit can appear only once in each run. Tap a square, then a digit on the number pad. Turn on Notes to pencil in candidates.

Is every puzzle really solvable without guessing?

Yes. Before a puzzle is included, our engine proves it has exactly one answer and solves it with logical steps only. If you are stuck, Hint outlines the next square and tells you which clue to look at.

I bought something but it's missing

Open Settings → Restore purchases (or the Store screen). Make sure you are signed in with the same Google or Apple account you used to buy. On Android, purchases also come back automatically the first time you open the app after reinstalling.

How do I remove ads?

Buy Remove Ads (a one-time purchase, never a subscription). It also gives unlimited hints.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it. Purchases are linked to your store account and can always be restored.

Text is too small or too large

Settings → Text size has three sizes, and High contrast makes everything bolder.
