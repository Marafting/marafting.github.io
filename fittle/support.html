Help & contact

How do I play?

Tap a square to choose its space (tap the same square again to switch between across and down). Then tap a word with the same number of letters. Every word fits exactly once. Start with lengths that have only one word, or spaces where only one word matches the letters already there.

Is every puzzle really solvable without guessing?

Yes. Before a puzzle is included, our engine proves it has exactly one solution and solves it with logical steps only. If you are stuck, Hint shows the next step and explains why.

I bought something but it's missing

Open Settings → Restore purchases (or the Store screen). Make sure you are signed in with the same Google or Apple account you used to buy. On Android, purchases also come back automatically the first time you open the app after reinstalling.

How do I remove ads?

Buy Remove Ads (a one-time purchase, never a subscription). It also gives unlimited hints.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it. Purchases are linked to your store account and can always be restored.

Text is too small or too large

Settings → Text size has three sizes, and High contrast makes everything bolder. Large grids can be enlarged with the Zoom button.
