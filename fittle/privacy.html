Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026. App: Fittle: Fill-In Word Puzzles (Android and iOS).

Fittle is made by Clear Clue Press, Sweden ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: no account, no analytics, and your puzzle progress never leaves your device. The only third parties are our advertising provider (Google AdMob) and the services that process in-app purchases.

1. Information stored on your device

Your puzzle progress, statistics, achievements and settings are saved only on your device. We do not receive them. You can delete them with Settings → Erase all progress, or by uninstalling the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads between puzzles, served by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect: device and advertising identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address and the approximate location derived from it, information about the ads you see and tap, and diagnostic information.

  • Your choice in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: before any ad is requested, the app shows Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices for ads.
  • iPhone and iPad: we do not ask for permission to track you and we request non-personalized ads only.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Google → Ads).
  • Buying Remove Ads stops all ads, and the advertising software is no longer started.

Google's use of this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your name, email or payment details. To confirm purchases and restore them on a new install, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives a random app user ID created on your device, purchase receipts and transaction details, and basic device information (for example operating system and app version). See revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. What we don't do

  • No sign-up, no accounts, no contact lists, no camera, no microphone, no precise location.
  • No analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • We do not sell personal information.

5. Children

Fittle is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to general-audience content.

6. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Personalized advertising is based on your consent. Non-personalized advertising, ad measurement and fraud prevention are based on our legitimate interest in funding a free app. Processing of purchases is necessary to provide what you bought.

7. Keeping and deleting data

We do not hold any data that identifies you. Data held by Google and RevenueCat is kept according to their policies. To ask about, access or delete purchase-related data, contact us (below) from the store account you used, and we will forward the request to the services involved.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to a data protection authority (in Sweden: Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY).

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
