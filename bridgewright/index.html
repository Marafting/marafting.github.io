Bridgewright

Calm hashi (bridges) puzzles with big, clear islands. Every puzzle can be solved by logic, with no guessing.

  • Big, clear islands and easy tapping, plus light, dark and high-contrast themes
  • Maps sized to your screen, with zoom for the big ones
  • A new daily puzzle, hints that explain the next step, no timers
  • Works offline. No account needed

Available on Google Play and the App Store.
