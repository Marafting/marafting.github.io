Help & contact

How do I play?

Join all the islands with bridges. Each island shows how many bridges it needs. Bridges run straight across or down between two islands, there are at most two bridges between the same two islands, and bridges never cross. In the end every island is connected to all the others. Tap an island, then a neighbour, to build a bridge; tap again for a second bridge and once more to remove them.

Is every puzzle really solvable without guessing?

Yes. Before a puzzle is included, our engine proves it has exactly one answer and solves it with logical steps only. If you are stuck, Hint outlines the island that decides the next bridge and explains why.

I bought something but it's missing

Open Settings → Restore purchases (or the Store screen). Make sure you are signed in with the same Google or Apple account you used to buy. On Android, purchases also come back automatically the first time you open the app after reinstalling.

How do I remove ads?

Buy Remove Ads (a one-time purchase, never a subscription). It also gives unlimited hints.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it. Purchases are linked to your store account and can always be restored.

Text is too small or too large

Settings → Text size has three sizes, and High contrast makes everything bolder.
